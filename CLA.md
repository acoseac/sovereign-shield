## How to sign

Contributions are gated by an automated CLA check on each pull request. When you open
your first PR, a bot will ask you to sign by posting a single comment on that PR with
exactly:

```
I have read the CLA and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, the commit reference, and a timestamp are recorded in the
`cla-signatures` branch of this repository as your electronic signature. This comment
constitutes a simple electronic signature within the meaning of Swiss law and forms a
binding agreement; those records are retained as evidence of Your agreement. You sign once;
the check passes automatically on future PRs. If this Agreement is materially revised, the
version number is incremented and you will be asked to sign the new version.
